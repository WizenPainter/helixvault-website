FAQ

Common

Still curious? Visit support →

Is HelixVault a medical device?+

No. HelixVault is a personal informational tracking app. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before acting on anything tracked in the app.

Where is my data stored?+

All data is stored locally on your device. You can optionally enable iCloud sync, in which case data syncs through your personal, end-to-end–encrypted iCloud account — we never have access to it.

Do I need an account?+

No. HelixVault does not use accounts, sign-ins, or remote user IDs. Open the app and start tracking.

What does Apple Health integration do?+

With your explicit permission, HelixVault reads HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, weight, and lean mass from Apple Health to let you correlate your protocol with outcomes. All reading is local; HealthKit data never leaves your device through HelixVault.
